The Host

Aidan Koh | Co-Founder & Lead Interviewer

Aidan drives the inquiry and guest curation for The Career Labs, architecting the conversations that dissect successful career pivots and industry realities. As a Merit Scholar studying Data Science and Economics at the National University of Singapore, his approach to career navigation is highly analytical.

Aidan specializes in cutting through noise to find actionable variables—a skill honed through four years of breaking down complex syllabi as an A-Level Economics tutor and tracking tech and semiconductor market cycles as an active proprietary trader.

That rigorous, data-first methodology is backed by the relentless discipline of 15 years as a national-level varsity swimmer. Aidan applies this same competitive drive to extract high-signal, unfiltered advice from industry leaders, building a definitive playbook for students, NSFs, and transitioning professionals.
